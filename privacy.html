Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

This policy covers the internal TikTok developer app n8n_forTiktok, used only by the StickBrief operator to publish StickBrief’s own educational videos to StickBrief’s own TikTok account.

Who we are

StickBrief is an educational YouTube Shorts channel (@StickBrief). This app is not a consumer product. It is not offered to the public.

What we collect

When the operator authorizes the app with TikTok Login Kit, TikTok may share:

  • basic public profile fields permitted by user.info.basic (display name, avatar, open_id)
  • authorization tokens needed to post videos with video.publish

We do not collect data from other TikTok users. We do not run ads. We do not sell data.

How we use it

Tokens are stored on the operator’s self-hosted n8n instance and used only to upload StickBrief videos to the authorizing TikTok account via TikTok’s Content Posting API.

Retention

Access tokens expire (typically 24 hours) and are refreshed with TikTok’s refresh-token flow. We delete tokens if authorization is revoked in TikTok settings.

Third parties

TikTok processes login and publishing under TikTok’s own terms. We do not send this data to other vendors.
